Partner roles

Roles open

Start where you fit. Each role gets quality leads, due-diligence support, compliant pathways, and written terms under NDA.

Role A

Call handlers

First-line qualification and warm handoff — keep the pipeline clean for closers and sourcers.

  • Quality inbound leads
  • Scripts and due-diligence checklists
  • Compliant workflow
  • Fair splits on attributed completions
Role B

Sales closers

Take qualified opportunities to agreed terms and completion. Structure and clarity — not hype.

  • Pre-qualified, mandate-fit leads
  • Deal analysis and due-diligence support
  • Investor access under NDA
  • Fee on completion — terms under NDA
Role C

Co-sourcers

Bring relationships and early-stage flow. Desk support on complex structures, paper, and process.

  • Desk capacity on complex structures
  • Due diligence and compliance scaffolding
  • Fair splits; economics locked per deal
